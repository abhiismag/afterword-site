Getting started

  1. Open Afterword and tap Record now. Choose My dictation for your own notes or Conversation for a conversation everyone agreed to record.
  2. Tap Finish conversation. Choose the person it was with, or skip for now.
  3. Review the transcript and the summary, untick anything wrong, and tap Save.
  4. Use Ask to question your saved conversations; answers show the source quotes they rest on.

Frequently asked questions

I recorded, but there is no summary. Open Models & privacy (gear icon). If your phone does not support Apple Intelligence, download Gemma and select Gemma reasoning on iPhone. Your recording and transcript are kept either way.

The transcript says "transcription needs iOS 26". Either update iOS, or download Whisper in Models & privacy; Whisper transcribes the saved recording on the phone.

Does the app send my recordings anywhere? No. Recordings, transcripts and memories stay on the phone. The only connections are model downloads you start and, if you set it up yourself, text sent to your own Mac. See the privacy policy.

How do I back up? Settings → Export creates a JSON file with your text records; export important recordings separately. The app's storage is excluded from iCloud and computer backups on purpose.

How do I delete my data? Delete a conversation or a person in Memory or People, or erase everything in Settings. Deleting the app removes all of its data.

Can it tell who is speaking? No. You choose the person a conversation belongs to. The app does not identify voices or detect emotions.

Recording stopped by itself. Recordings stop when the app leaves the foreground unless you switched on Keep recording when screen locks before starting, on a phone call, on a headset change, and at the 20-minute limit. The audio captured so far is kept.

Known limitations in this version

  • Transcription quality varies by language, accent and background noise; Hindi, Bengali and mixed-language speech are less accurate than English and German.
  • Summaries are AI interpretations and can be wrong; always check the quoted source.
  • No cloud sync between devices.
